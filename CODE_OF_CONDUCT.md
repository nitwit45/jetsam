# Code of conduct

This covers the issues, pull requests and discussions of this repository.

- Argue about the code, the bug or the idea. Leave the person out of it.
- No harassment, insults, slurs or sexual content, and no posting of anyone's private information.
- Someone reporting that Jetsam removed their files gets a polite answer, whoever turns out to be at fault.

I maintain Jetsam alone and moderate it alone. I hide or delete what breaks these rules and block accounts that keep doing it.

To report a comment, use **Report content** in its menu, which goes to GitHub. To reach me without posting in public, open a private report under [Security > Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/nitwit45/jetsam/security/advisories/new) and say it is about conduct.
